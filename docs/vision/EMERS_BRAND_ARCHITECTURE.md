## 2. Brand overview

EMERS is the working brand family for disciplined investment decision support,
risk analysis, and controlled broker interaction. The brand should feel calm,
precise, trustworthy, modern, and responsible.

## 3. Name origin

EMERS is named for Eduardo, Macarena, Emilia, Raimundo, and Santiago. The origin
should communicate responsibility, permanence, trust, disciplined
decision-making, long-term thinking, and the standard of building something
worthy of one's own family.

## 4. Brand hierarchy

| Level | Working name | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Organization | EMERS Technologies | Company or organization identity |
| Primary product | EMERS Trade | Customer-facing trading decision and execution platform |
| Technology engine | EduTraderAI Engine | Existing deterministic trading and risk technology |
| Presentation | EMERS Trade, Powered by EduTraderAI | Possible transitional product language |

## 5. Naming conventions

Use EMERS Technologies for the organization, EMERS Trade for the product, and
EduTraderAI Engine for the technology layer when distinguishing brand from
implementation. Do not rename source modules during brand migration without a
controlled compatibility plan.

## 6. Brand personality

EMERS should be:

- Intelligent.
- Disciplined.
- Calm.
- Trustworthy.
- Modern.
- Precise.
- Transparent.
- Responsible.
- Internationally understandable.
- Approachable without appearing casual.

EMERS should avoid appearing aggressive, casino-like, excessively luxurious,
sensational, mysterious, overly technical, profit-obsessed, fear-driven, or
urgency-driven.

## 7. Voice and tone

EMERS language should be direct, calm, factual, understandable, respectful,
transparent about uncertainty, precise about risk, and free from exaggerated
claims.

Preferred language includes:

- "The available evidence supports..."
- "This scenario involves..."
- "Maximum planned loss..."
- "The broker has acknowledged the order."
- "This result remains uncertain."
- "Operator approval is required."

Avoid:

- "Guaranteed winner."
- "Easy money."
- "Cannot lose."
- "The AI knows."
- "Act now before it is too late."
- "Risk-free profit."
- "Beat the market automatically."

## 9. Product language

Use language that distinguishes current capability from future vision. For
current capability, describe deterministic Paper workflows, risk controls,
explainability, broker-state reconciliation, and release evidence only where
validated. For future capability, use terms such as "planned," "directional,"
"potential," or "subject to validation."

## 10. Prohibited claims

Do not claim guaranteed profits, risk-free trading, automatic market beating,
legal or regulated adviser status, broker status, trademark ownership, public
availability, or current support for future capabilities that have not been
implemented and validated.

## 11. Visual direction

The visual direction should support calm clarity: restrained colors, clear
hierarchy, readable typography, disciplined spacing, visible risk states, and
plain-language confirmation. It should avoid casino cues, urgency patterns,
excessive luxury styling, and visuals that imply certainty.

## 12. Legal and availability review

Before public commercial adoption, the EMERS name and associated marks must
undergo:

- Trademark search.
- Company-name availability review.
- Domain availability review.
- App-store name review.
- Social-handle review.
- Conflict and confusion analysis.
- Financial-services naming review.
- Applicable regulatory and legal review.

This document does not claim that EMERS, EMERS Technologies, or EMERS Trade is
currently available or legally protected.

## 13. Migration from EduTraderAI

Phase 1: Internal technical name remains EduTraderAI.

Phase 2: User-facing interface introduces EMERS Trade, Powered by EduTraderAI.

Phase 3: Technical modules may gradually adopt EMERS naming only through
controlled, backward-compatible changes.

Phase 4: Public launch occurs only after legal, security, product, broker, and
regulatory readiness.

Source modules must not be renamed during this brand-architecture activity.
