## 2. Purpose

This document describes a five-year aspiration for EMERS. It is directional. It
is not a product commitment, legal representation, regulatory conclusion, or
release schedule.

## 3. Starting position

The current foundation includes:

- EduTraderAI v4.0.0 Stable authorized.
- 390 tests passing.
- Formal architecture validation.
- Immutable evidence process.
- Operational validation campaign.
- Seven-day observation.
- Alpaca Paper broker lifecycle demonstrated.
- v4.1 roadmap initialized.
- Current private single-user and single-process model.
- Documented limitations and follow-up work.

## 4. Year-one objective

Year one should focus on deterministic Paper qualification, stronger
coordination, event-delivery observability, formal performance thresholds,
release automation, EMERS user-facing identity, improved interface and
usability, secure deployment design, initial web-application architecture, and
supported-broker research.

## 5. Years two and three

Potential directions for years two and three include secure web platform, user
authentication, mobile monitoring and approvals, portfolio analytics, alerts,
configurable risk policies, supported broker integrations, cloud deployment,
multi-user architecture, and durable audit and event infrastructure.

Each direction is subject to validation and legal or regulatory review.

## 6. Years four and five

The longer-term aspiration is that EMERS becomes a trusted international platform
for disciplined investment decision support, portfolio understanding, controlled
execution, and transparent evidence.

It should be known not for promising extraordinary profits, but for helping users
make better-governed decisions.

## 7. Product evolution

EMERS Trade may evolve from a private Paper-first tool into a broader decision
platform with web and mobile access, configurable policies, portfolio-level
analytics, and supported broker integrations. Every expansion should preserve
risk-first presentation, human control, and evidence-backed operation.

## 8. Technology evolution

EduTraderAI may evolve into a more modular engine with stronger qualification
mode, cross-process coordination, durable event publication, versioned evidence
schemas, clearer APIs, and formal performance gates. Technical evolution should
be backward-compatible where practical and migration-aware where compatibility
cannot be preserved.

## 9. Trust and governance evolution

Trust should deepen through repeatable release qualification, redacted evidence,
incident discipline, clear limitations, verified broker workflows, and product
language that avoids unsupported claims.

## 10. Commercial possibilities

Commercial possibilities may include private subscription access, managed web
hosting, professional decision-support tools, broker-supported integrations, and
team workflows. These are possibilities only. They require separate validation,
security review, legal analysis, regulatory analysis, and product-market
research.

## 11. Regulatory and legal dependencies

EMERS is not automatically a registered investment adviser, broker, exchange,
bank, fund manager, or guarantor of investment performance. Future commercial
operation may require specialist legal and regulatory analysis in every relevant
jurisdiction. No definitive legal conclusion is made here.

## 12. Measures of progress

Progress should be measured by safer broker qualification, fewer unresolved
failure modes, clearer user understanding, validated coordination, observable
event delivery, evidence integrity, release reliability, compatibility, and
trustworthy presentation.

## 13. Risks to the vision

Key risks include premature live-trading expansion, legal or trademark conflict,
unclear regulatory posture, weak coordination in multi-process deployments,
insufficient event durability, inflated product claims, and user-interface
patterns that create pressure rather than discipline.

## 14. What must remain unchanged

Regardless of size or commercial success:

- No guaranteed-profit claims.
- No hidden risk.
- No implicit live trading.
- No surrender of meaningful human control.
- No silent broker failures.
- No misleading AI certainty.
- No degradation of auditability.
- No release without evidence.
- No commercial objective above user safety and trust.

## 15. Five-year aspiration

When a person is considering an important investment decision, EMERS should be
the platform they trust to help them understand the opportunity, examine the
downside, control the risk, and act with discipline.
