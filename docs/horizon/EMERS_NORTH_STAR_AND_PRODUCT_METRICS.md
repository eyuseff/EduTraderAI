# EMERS North Star and Product Metrics

## 1. Purpose

Define product measurement direction without turning engagement into the product goal.

## 2. Measurement philosophy

Measure decision quality, safety, clarity, reliability, and evidence, not trading frequency.

## 3. Proposed North Star

Percentage of material investment decisions completed with documented risk analysis and explicit user approval.

"Material investment decision" requires later operational definition.

## 4. Supporting product metrics

Trade plans with complete risk fields, broker actions with explicit authorization, orders reconciled to final broker state, unresolved broker-event age, duplicate-submission incidents, stale-data actions prevented, safety-gate failure rate, alert acknowledgement time, evidence completeness, approval abandonment after risk review, decisions later reviewed, accessibility defect rate, critical-error recovery success, and user understanding of risk/status.

## 5. Safety metrics

Duplicate submissions, unresolved broker states, unsafe configuration blocks, stale-data blocks, and policy rejections.

## 6. Decision-quality metrics

Risk-field completeness, review completion, plan adherence, and post-trade review completion.

## 7. Reliability metrics

Verification pass rate, broker-status retrieval, evidence generation, alert delivery, and recovery success.

## 8. Broker metrics

Acknowledgment rate, reconciliation rate, cancellation confirmation, unresolved order age, and broker error rate.

## 9. Experience metrics

Risk comprehension, task completion, error recovery, accessibility defects, and support incidents.

## 10. Commercial metrics

Commercial metrics are future and must not override safety.

## 11. Anti-metrics

Do not make primary success measures out of number of trades, trading frequency, time spent in app, notification opens, profit without risk adjustment, volume submitted, impulse conversions, or engagement created through urgency.

## 12. Measurement risks

Risks include gaming, pressure to trade, privacy overreach, and false confidence.

## 13. Data requirements

Metrics require clean definitions, consent where needed, redaction, and provenance.

## 14. Governance

Metric changes should be reviewed against EMERS principles.

## 15. Open decisions

Open decisions include operational definition, dashboard placement, retention, and pilot measurement method.
