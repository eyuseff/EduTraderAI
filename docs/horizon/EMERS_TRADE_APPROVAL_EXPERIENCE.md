# EMERS Trade Approval Experience

## 1. Purpose

Define how EMERS presents consequential order authorization.

## 2. Approval doctrine

Approval must be explicit, informed, and tied to one material action.

## 3. Preconditions

Valid plan, current data status, broker environment, risk results, and operator identity/authority where applicable.

## 4. Approval-screen hierarchy

Environment, broker, instrument, side, order type, quantity, capital, entry, stop, target, maximum planned loss, portfolio impact, rationale, uncertainty, acknowledgements, action.

## 5. Instrument and side

Instrument and BUY/SELL must be unmistakable.

## 6. Quantity and capital

Show quantity and estimated capital before approval.

## 7. Entry, stop, and target

Show order type, entry condition or price, stop, and target.

## 8. Risk summary

Show maximum planned loss, exposure after approval, concentration, reward/risk, and policy state.

## 9. Portfolio impact

Show exposure and concentration after approval.

## 10. Assumptions

Show assumptions and missing data.

## 11. Recommendation explanation

Explain principal reasons without claiming certainty.

## 12. Data freshness

Display timestamps and stale-data warnings.

## 13. Broker and environment

Show broker and environment: simulation, Paper, or future live.

## 14. Required acknowledgements

Acknowledge broker action, risk, environment, and unresolved uncertainty where relevant.

## 15. Approval language

For Paper, use: AUTHORIZE PAPER ORDER. Do not use vague labels such as Continue, Proceed, Okay, or Confirm.

## 16. Modification

Modification should return to planning and invalidate stale approval.

## 17. Rejection

Rejection should preserve reason and no broker order should be created.

## 18. Submission

Submission begins only after explicit authorization and must show pending state.

## 19. Evidence

Evidence should record plan, risk, approval, broker request, broker response, and correlation ID.

## 20. Failure and recovery

Failures require visible status, blocked unsafe actions, and reconciliation path.

## 21. Future live-trading requirements

Future live mode requires separately designed and approved safeguards.

## 22. Open decisions

Open decisions include exact acknowledgement text, layout, mobile approval policy, and identity requirements.
