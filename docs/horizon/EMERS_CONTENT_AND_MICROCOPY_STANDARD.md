# EMERS Content and Microcopy Standard

## 1. Purpose

Define product language that is calm, precise, and honest.

## 2. Voice

Calm, direct, precise, respectful, factual, transparent about uncertainty, and specific about consequences.

## 3. Tone

Professional and plain-spoken; never sensational.

## 4. Terminology

Use preview, plan, risk, approval, submission, acknowledgment, fill, cancellation, rejection, unresolved, reconciliation, Paper, and evidence precisely.

## 5. Status language

Say exactly what state is known. Do not call an order completed unless final broker state supports it.

## 6. Risk language

Use maximum planned loss, exposure, risk limit, concentration, stale data, and unresolved uncertainty.

## 7. Recommendation language

Use: The available evidence supports further review. This recommendation includes unresolved uncertainty.

## 8. Approval language

For Paper use: AUTHORIZE PAPER ORDER. Avoid Continue, Proceed, Okay, and Confirm for material actions.

## 9. Broker language

Preferred: The broker acknowledged the order. The order has not filled. Cancellation has been requested but not yet confirmed.

## 10. Error language

Errors should name impact and safe next step.

## 11. Warning language

Warnings should explain consequence, not dramatize.

## 12. Empty states

Empty states should explain what is absent and what safe next step exists.

## 13. Success messages

Success should be factual: Evidence exported. Broker acknowledged. Order cancelled.

## 14. AI-generated content

AI text must disclose uncertainty and not invent missing data.

## 15. Localization

Localization should preserve risk and legal nuance.

## 16. Prohibited language

Avoid: Great trade! Guaranteed winner. Easy profit. Act now. You are missing out. The AI is certain. Order completed when only submitted. Safe without precise context. Everything is fine when important state is unavailable.

## 17. Review checklist

Does the text show state, risk, uncertainty, consequence, and source? Does it avoid hype?

## 18. Examples

Preferred examples: The broker acknowledged the order. Market data is delayed. This scenario exceeds the configured risk limit. Operator authorization is required.

## 19. Governance

Material microcopy should be reviewed when it affects broker actions, risk, or legal posture.

## 20. Open decisions

Open decisions include glossary, Spanish localization, tone variants, and support-script language.
