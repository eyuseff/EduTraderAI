EMERS Technologies is the working organization identity, EMERS Trade is the working customer-facing product identity, and EduTraderAI Engine is the working technology-engine identity. These names remain subject to legal, trademark, domain, company-name, and regulatory review.

## 2. Purpose

Project Atlas is a strategic and architectural blueprint for transforming the validated EduTraderAI engine into the future EMERS Trade ecosystem without weakening safety, human control, transparency, auditability, or release discipline.

## 3. Relationship to the EMERS Constitution

Atlas inherits the EMERS Constitution: safety before speed, risk before return, human judgment, transparency, evidence, privacy, and release quality. Atlas must not overrule those principles.

## 4. Strategic proposition

We are not in the business of predicting markets. We are in the business of helping people make better decisions. EMERS Trade should help users understand opportunity, downside, exposure, uncertainty, and broker state before acting.

## 5. Current starting point

The starting point is EduTraderAI v4.0.0 Stable: deterministic Paper workflows, supervised scanner execution, evidence governance, operator approval, local audit artifacts, Alpaca Paper lifecycle evidence, and documented single-process limitations.

## 6. Desired destination

The desired destination is a secure EMERS Trade platform with disciplined user experience, validated broker qualification, explicit identity and authorization, durable evidence, observable events, careful data governance, and staged delivery.

## 7. Atlas workstreams

- Product experience.
- Product architecture.
- Technology architecture.
- Security and trust.
- Data and intelligence.
- Broker integrations.
- Cloud and deployment evolution.
- Mobile experience.
- Commercial readiness.
- Legal and regulatory dependencies.
- Staged delivery.
- Operational governance.

## 8. Non-goals

Atlas does not authorize implementation, change approved v4.1 engineering priorities, select vendors, launch commercially, enable live trading, create broker integrations, or make legal or regulatory conclusions.

## 9. Constraints

Current constraints include Paper-first operation, no implicit live trading, single-process deployment until coordination is validated, non-durable event publishing until replaced, explicit operator approval, and evidence-backed release gates.

## 10. Decision principles

Prefer safety over speed, explicit contracts over assumptions, durable state before multi-process deployment, official broker APIs only, evidence before release, user control before convenience, and measurable benefit before complexity.

## 11. Major dependencies

Major dependencies include v4.1 qualification work, cross-process coordination decisions, event publisher observability, security review, broker API research, legal and regulatory analysis, and evidence-schema versioning.

## 12. Risks

Risks include premature commercialization, unclear regulatory obligations, security weaknesses, accidental live routing, distributed coordination gaps, hidden broker-state failures, misleading AI language, and overbuilding before evidence.

## 13. Measures of progress

Progress should be measured by validated safety gates, evidence integrity, fewer unresolved operational risks, clearer user understanding, tested qualification workflows, visible event delivery, and preserved v4.0 compatibility.

## 14. Governance

Atlas decisions should be documented through ADRs or decision-register entries. Unresolved decisions must remain marked as research required, undecided, or deferred.

## 15. Immediate next steps

The next approved implementation item remains V41-PQ-001: design the Paper qualification state machine. Atlas implementation beyond that requires separate approval.
