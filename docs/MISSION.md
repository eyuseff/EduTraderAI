# EduTraderAI Mission

EduTraderAI is a deterministic, explainable trading research and execution platform designed for disciplined personal use.

Its purpose is to help the user:

- research trading strategies,
- replay historical market data,
- evaluate trading ideas,
- simulate execution,
- manage portfolio risk,
- document every decision,
- and eventually support carefully controlled live trading.

Artificial intelligence may generate observations, hypotheses, and trade recommendations. It must never bypass deterministic risk controls or directly execute orders.

## Mission Statement

> EduTraderAI transforms market information into reproducible, explainable, and risk-controlled trading decisions.

## Core Objectives

EduTraderAI must:

1. Protect capital before pursuing returns.
2. Separate strategy decisions from execution.
3. Keep risk management deterministic.
4. Make every trade auditable.
5. Reproduce results from identical inputs.
6. Support interchangeable market feeds, strategies, brokers, and analytics.
7. Remain understandable to its owner.
8. Favor reliability over speed of development.
9. Validate every production feature with automated tests.
10. Evolve toward live trading only after historical and paper-trading behavior is trusted.

## Intended Use

EduTraderAI is designed primarily as a personal trading research and decision-support platform.

It is not intended to:

- guarantee profits,
- eliminate market risk,
- replace professional financial advice,
- execute unreviewed AI decisions,
- or conceal uncertainty behind automated outputs.

## Long-Term Vision

EduTraderAI should become a personal trading operating system that can:

- analyze market conditions,
- run deterministic strategies,
- receive AI-assisted recommendations,
- apply independent Guardian controls,
- calculate position size,
- enforce portfolio limits,
- execute through replaceable brokers,
- maintain a complete ledger,
- and explain every accepted or rejected decision.
